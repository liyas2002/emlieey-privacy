Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. About emLee

emLee is an AI-powered YouTube live chat assistant created and owned by Liyas (Lee). It is designed to interact with viewers in live chat and provide short, conversational responses.

2. Information Processed

Depending on how emLee is used, the system may process publicly visible YouTube live-chat information such as usernames, messages, commands and interaction data.

3. How Information Is Used

  • to respond to live-chat messages
  • to provide commands and interactive features
  • to maintain conversational context
  • to improve the behavior and reliability of emLee
  • to prevent spam and misuse

4. AI Processing

Some messages may be processed by third-party AI services to generate responses. The exact AI provider may change as the system is updated.

5. YouTube

emLee interacts with YouTube through available YouTube APIs and live-chat functionality. YouTube's own policies and privacy practices may also apply.

6. Viewer Preferences & Memories

emLee may store limited conversational preferences, nicknames or interaction-related information when required for the bot's functionality and continuity.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the functionality, security and operation of emLee, subject to applicable platform and service limitations.

8. Security

Reasonable technical measures may be used to protect stored information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

emLee is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public live chat.

10. Information You Should Not Share

Do not share passwords, payment information, private addresses, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information through live chat.

11. Your Choices

Users may choose what information they share in public chat. Where applicable, requests concerning stored information can be directed to the creator.

12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when emLee's features, services or technical architecture change.
