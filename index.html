privacy policy

how Emlieey handles community information

1. information we collect

Emlieey may store information such as a username, points, XP, level, game statistics, streak information, achievements and activity required to provide community features.

2. AI conversations

Messages submitted through the Emlieey website may be processed by the Emlieey backend to generate AI responses. The backend may use Groq AI services for response generation.

3. community data

Points, XP, levels, games, achievements and leaderboard information may be stored in Cloudflare D1 to provide persistent community functionality.

4. cookies and local storage

The website may use browser local storage for basic preferences such as the currently selected username. You can clear local storage through your browser settings.

5. third-party services

Emlieey may rely on services including Cloudflare, Groq and YouTube. Their own privacy policies may apply when their services process information.

6. security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the backend and community data. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Emlieey, contact the creator through the official social accounts listed on this website.

terms of use

rules for using the Emlieey community

1. acceptable use

Do not use Emlieey for unlawful activity, harassment, abuse, spam, impersonation or attempts to disrupt the service.

2. community points

Points, XP, levels, achievements and leaderboard positions are virtual community features. They have no cash value unless explicitly stated.

3. games

Emlieey games are entertainment features. Results may be generated by server-side or client-side logic depending on the feature.

4. AI responses

AI-generated responses may sometimes be incorrect, incomplete or unexpected. Do not treat Emlieey as a professional or authoritative source.

5. availability

Features may be changed, temporarily unavailable or removed as the Emlieey project develops.

6. changes

These terms and the website features may be updated from time to time.
