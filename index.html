Privacy Policy

1. Information We May Process

Emlieey may process limited information necessary for its functionality, including:

  • YouTube public channel or profile information
  • Public YouTube display name or handle
  • Messages voluntarily sent in live chat
  • Commands or interactions sent to Emlieey
  • Limited conversation context
  • Viewer preferences or nicknames when enabled
  • Technical information provided by third-party platforms

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Emlieey is strictly used for:

  • Responding to live-chat messages
  • Understanding conversation context
  • Providing commands and interactive games
  • Remembering viewer preferences or nicknames when enabled
  • Improving reliability and behavior
  • Preventing spam, abuse, or misuse
  • Troubleshooting and maintaining the service

3. AI Processing

Emlieey uses third-party AI services to generate real-time responses. Limited chat messages and conversation context may be transmitted to an AI provider when necessary to generate a response. Users should not send sensitive personal information to the bot.

4. YouTube Integration

Emlieey interacts with YouTube live-chat functionality through official Google/YouTube services and APIs. YouTube operates under its own Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and data practices. Emlieey does not control YouTube's data handling.

5. Third-Party Services

Emlieey relies on third-party providers for technical functionality including AI generation, API integrations, hosting, infrastructure, and authentication. Third-party providers operate under their own independent terms and privacy policies.

6. Viewer Memories and Preferences

When enabled, Emlieey may remember limited viewer information such as preferred nicknames, relevant conversation context, and certain interaction preferences. This is designed solely to enhance the live-chat experience and does not create a sensitive personal profile.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for operational functionality, security, troubleshooting, and service integrity.

8. Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. However, no internet-based service or data transmission can guarantee 100% security.

9. Children's Privacy

Emlieey is not intentionally designed to collect sensitive personal information from children. Users are strongly advised not to share personal or sensitive details in public live chat environments.

10. Information Users Should NOT Share

Please refrain from submitting any sensitive data to live chat, including but not limited to:

  • Passwords or account credentials
  • OTP / two-factor authentication codes
  • Credit/debit card information
  • Bank account details
  • Government ID numbers
  • Private home addresses
  • Any highly sensitive personal information

11. User Choices

Users can freely choose not to interact with Emlieey and should avoid voluntarily sharing personal or sensitive information within the live chat.

12. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect service changes. The latest version will always be accessible directly on this page.
