Privacy Policy

last updated: october 7, 2026

1. overview

emLee is a community space around Emlieey Live. This policy explains what information may be used when you use the website and its community features.

2. information you provide

You may provide a username, profile picture, messages, and other content when using emLee. Your profile picture may be stored locally in your browser for your profile experience.

3. community features

Features such as live chat, private viewer chat, AI chat, profiles, rankings, rewards, and games may process the information needed to provide those features. Messages and profile-related information may be sent to the emLee backend when a feature requires it.

4. local storage

emLee may use browser local storage to remember settings and community information such as your username, profile picture selection, chat contacts, unread counts, and game-related local state.

5. AI chat

When you use the emLee AI chat feature, your message may be sent through the emLee backend to provide an AI-generated response. Do not submit passwords, payment information, exact addresses, or other highly sensitive private information.

6. third-party services

emLee may rely on hosting, backend, AI, or other technical service providers to operate its features. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

7. security

Reasonable technical measures are used to operate the service, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use the community responsibly and avoid sharing sensitive information.

8. children

emLee is not intended to be a place for children to share personal information. If you are under the applicable age for online services in your location, use the service only with appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

9. changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its features change. The latest version will be published on this page.
